## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an immersive, theater-grade experience tailored for late-night media discovery and mood-driven curation. Designed for film enthusiasts and casual viewers seeking emotionally resonant recommendations, the interface evokes the quiet anticipation of a darkened cinema hall pierced by luminous projector light.

The aesthetic bridges refined editorial minimalism with subtle, glowing glassmorphism. Surfaces remain predominantly ultra-dark, allowing vibrant film artwork and luminous atmospheric accents to take center stage. Interfaces maintain high contrast, tactile feedback, and seamless visual transitions to preserve focus and visual comfort in dim environments.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by deep obsidian and midnight tones that establish an uncompromisingly dark canvas, preventing eye strain and framing visual content with high contrast.

- **Primary (`#FFB800`)**: Electric amber gold used for primary call-to-actions, featured ratings, curated mood highlights, and primary active states.
- **Secondary (`#8B5CF6`)**: Moody deep violet applied to ambient backdrops, mood indicators, and secondary highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#A855F7`)**: Radiant amethyst reserved for tertiary accents, interactive gradient stops, and glow states.
- **Neutral (`#0A0A0F`)**: Obsidian void base surface, layered with `#12121A` (surface elevated) and `#1A1A24` (surface container).
- **Text & Borders**: Primary text resolves to `#F8FAFC` for stark readability, secondary metadata to `#94A3B8`, and structural borders to translucent white washes (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.14)`).

## Typography

The type scale combines an expressive, architectural display face with an ultra-legible, geometric sans-serif:

- **Headlines (`Syne`)**: Features bold theatrical shapes, wide proportions, and distinctive cuts that invoke film titles and cinematic editorial posters.
- **Body & Labels (`Outfit`)**: Clean, balanced, and open geometry that maintains sharp optical clarity on dark backgrounds across compact viewports.
- Line heights are tuned generously to avoid visual crowding against deep obsidian backgrounds. Upper-case transformation with letter-spacing is applied strictly to compact badges, movie tags, and runtimes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy uses a flexible, mobile-first column system designed to showcase edge-to-edge cinematic imagery alongside safe ergonomic margins.

- **Grid Model**: A standard 4-column fluid layout on mobile viewports (breakpoint `< 600px`), transitioning to an 8-column layout on small tablets (`600px - 1024px`) and 12-column on larger viewports.
- **Margins & Safe Zones**: Native mobile screens enforce a `1.25rem` (`20px`) lateral canvas margin. Hero banners and media carousels can bleed full-bleed horizontally, using inner padding tokens to snap content back to the visual gutter.
- **Component Rhythm**: Internal stack spacing strictly adheres to the 4px/8px modular base scale (`space-xs` through `space-xl`). Posters, metadata blocks, and interactive mood trays maintain a minimum `space-md` (`16px`) separation to avoid visual collision.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is built through tiered obsidian surfaces, translucent frosted blurs, and luminous chromatic edge glow:

- **Surface Tiers**:
  - `Level 0 (Canvas)`: `#0A0A0F` flat obsidian foundation.
  - `Level 1 (Card/Surface)`: `#12121A` with a 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`.
  - `Level 2 (Overlay/Modal)`: `#1A1A24` at 85% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and an ambient shadow `0 16px 32px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.75)`.
- **Atmospheric Glows**: Interactive or emphasized states emit soft outer radial glows:
  - Amber highlights utilize `0 0 24px -4px rgba(255, 184, 0, 0.35)`.
  - Violet mood accents project `0 0 28px -4px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.30)`.
- **Frosted Glass Borders**: Cards and floating navigation bars rely on a linear gradient stroke (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)` top-left to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)` bottom-right) to mimic physical glass catches against light sources.

## Shapes

The interface balances sharp cinematic ratios with refined ergonomic curvatures:

- **Base Radius (`roundedness: 2`)**: Content cards, sheet surfaces, dialog modals, and media frames share an 8px (`0.5rem`) to 16px (`1rem`) corner radius.
- **Poster Cards**: Adhere strictly to the standard **2:3 aspect ratio** for main discovery grids, and **16:9** for horizontal backdrops. Corners are clipped smoothly at `12px` to prevent clipping poster corner details.
- **Interactive Controls**: Pill-shapes (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) are reserved for high-engagement touch elements, including mood selector chips, play buttons, and tag badges.

## Components

- **Buttons**:
  - *Primary*: Solid `#FFB800` fill, `#0A0A0F` bold typography, pill-shaped radius. Active state produces a subtle amber bloom shadow.
  - *Secondary / Glass*: `#1A1A24` background with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, crisp `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` border, and `#F8FAFC` label.
  - *Icon Actions*: Circular frosted buttons (`44px` minimum hit target) positioned over media surfaces with 40% dark overlay.

- **Mood Selector Chips**:
  - Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) horizontal scrolling elements.
  - *Inactive*: `#12121A` fill, `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` border, muted `#94A3B8` text.
  - *Active*: Deep violet-to-amethyst gradient fill (`#8B5CF6` to `#A855F7`), white text, and an outer amethyst ambient blur (`0 0 16px rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.4)`).

- **Cards (Poster & Recommendation)**:
  - Standard 2:3 vertical posters featuring subtle vignette gradients overlaying the bottom 40% of the image to keep mood indicators and match percentages legible.
  - Border uses a frosted glass catch line; tap states trigger a slight scale transition (`scale: 0.98`) with luminous border brightening.

- **Badges & Metadata**:
  - Ultra-compact pills carrying movie ratings (e.g., "★ 8.9"), content warnings, or duration tokens.
  - Rendered in `#1A1A24` at 90% opacity, crisp 1px borders, and yellow/amber accent icons.

- **Form Fields & Inputs**:
  - Dark search bars (`#12121A`) with inset borders, pill curvature, and glowing amber focus rings (`0 0 0 2px rgba(255, 184, 0, 0.4)`).
  - Placeholder text styled in subdued slate (`#64748B`).

- **Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)**:
  - Rounded squares for checkboxes (`rounded-sm`), circular for radios. Unchecked states use `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` borders. Checked states fill with solid `#FFB800` with dark iconography.
